What is Man? What makes Man? Is man just a construct, a complex multilayered structure built on top of immaterial things such as a soul or a mind? Is man just a body?

Are we made of the things we feel, we touch, we see, and we experience? Do they define us?

If you say yes, why do you say yes? I then ask, "Are your experiences truly yours or those forced upon you by a society or a world you were brought into?"

What's the difference between you and I? We feel the same things, don't we? You feel the pain, loss, grief, defeat, failure, and rejection I feel, and you also feel the joy, beauty, admiration, happiness, and excitement I feel as well, don't you? That's why we are friends, isn't it? Because in the identity, personality, masculinity, beliefs, and social roles, we have found a common ground, you and I, haven't we? Do you feel different when I'm not there to share in those moments? When you are suffocated, overwhelmed with emotions, or drowned in a profound wave of nauseating affections, and I'm not there, do you feel something missing, or does it remind you of your humanity? Does it solidify your personality, your self-identity?

With 1000 questions, I apologize for starting off with such a tone. I would love to hear your answers to them, no matter how imperfect they might be.

This year, an emotion I have truly come to appreciate and deeply admire is disappointment, disappointment borne from failure, rejection, and expectations. What's the difference between all of these, you might ask? Failure is an outcome, rejection is an outcome, and disappointment is the emotion after the outcome of the action you took. I have been disappointed so much this year, but I've learned to pick myself up and go again and to keep going, no matter how many times I fall, to keep fighting against every undesirable outcome. Even with the pain, you can't always change the feeling, but you can change the outcome. The pain would always be there, but the outcome can be different.

And so, I have come to implore you, my friend, to fight, to rage against the storm, to fight tooth and nail for the sliver of hope, for the slightest chance that the outcome might be different, even if it never had been different, even if it costs you your humanity. Would it cost you something? Yes, it would. And you might not be given the chance to choose what the cost might be, so I implore you to take as much from life as it dares to take from you. Remember, "Every day is another day to fight hard for your life," "Rage, rage against the dying of the light" (Dylan Thomas, *Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night*).

What is Man? Man is the only animal that refuses to be what he is.

Happy new week to you, my friend. May the new week usher you into a higher place to receive of the blessings of thine God and to function in the capacity and purpose you were made for
